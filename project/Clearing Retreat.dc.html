01 — The idea

Two days, set aside. Conversations about where artificial intelligence is taking us — and the older questions underneath — held slowly, in good company.

Between the conversations: meditation, unhurried meals, a good bottle of wine, and a quiet place to walk. You arrive in the gears of everyday work. You leave with a little more clarity than you came with.

02 — What makes it different

Four things rarely sit at the same table: senior judgment about technology, philosophy, a contemplative practice, and genuine hospitality.

Most retreats offer one of them. A leadership offsite has the strategy but not the silence. A meditation week has the silence but not the substance. A good dinner has the warmth but no through-line. This holds all four at once, and lets them change one another over two days.

It is not a course, and not a conference. It is a small group of people who think for a living, given the time and the setting to think well, together.
