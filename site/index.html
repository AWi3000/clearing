01 — The idea

Two days, set aside. Clearing borrows the shape of a long wine pairing — a run of courses, each with its wine and its story — and makes it a vessel for thinking.

The days are for clarity: walking, stillness in three forms — sitting, walking, standing — and conversation about where artificial intelligence is taking us, over light meals. The evenings are for the table: a pairing, course by course, where the day’s questions come together over a glass and a story. You arrive in the gears of everyday work; you leave with a little more clarity than you came with.

02 — What makes it different

Four things rarely sit at the same table: senior judgment about technology, philosophy, a contemplative practice, and genuine hospitality.

Most retreats offer one of them. A leadership offsite has the strategy but not the silence. A meditation week has the silence but not the substance. A good dinner has the warmth but no through-line. The pairing is what holds all four at one table — course by course, each thread tasted and considered — and lets them change one another over two days.

It is not a course, and not a conference, but a small group of people who think for a living, given the time to think well together. The wine is never the point and never the excess — it is the ritual that slows the talk down, each bottle with a story rather than a tally.
